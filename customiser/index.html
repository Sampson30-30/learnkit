About this page

What this is. A theme is a complete look: colours, corners and fonts. Here you start from one and change it. The preview updates straight away. Your changes are kept in this browser, on this device only.

What you can do here

  • Pick a look to start from, then change its colours. Light and dark have their own colours.
  • Change how rounded the corners are, how much space there is, and the fonts.
  • Watch the Contrast panel. Contrast means how clearly text stands out from its background. It tells you if some people would find your colours hard to read.
  • When you are happy, copy or download your changes and give them to Claude, or to whoever looks after the code.

About this page

What this is. A shop of building blocks. Each block does one job, such as a quiz or a timeline. You do not need to build the blocks yourself: you can ask Claude to use them.

What you can do here

  • Browse the library below. Every piece is working, so you can try it.
  • Compare looks in the Theme gallery. A theme is a complete look: colours, corners and fonts.
  • Make a look of your own in the Customiser.
  • Find out what works inside a Rise course, with the Rise test.

The area at the bottom is for the person who looks after the code. You can skip it.

What LearnKit does

  • Ready-made pieces

    Building blocks for what learning content needs: checking understanding, telling a story, organising information and getting people to reflect.

  • Looks you can swap

    Every piece can wear any of the looks, or one you make yourself. Change the look without touching the content.

  • Made for everyone

    Every piece works with a keyboard and a screen reader, in light and dark. Colours are checked so text stays easy to read.

  • Nothing to install

    Each piece works on its own. There is no extra software to set up, and nothing to keep updating.
